How this page works

  1. Chunk & tag

    Every résumé line is a chunk, tagged with the entities it mentions — roles, capabilities, domains. The tags become the graph's edges.

  2. Lexical first

    A hand-rolled BM25 index answers instantly, before anything downloads.

  3. Semantic on demand

    On your first question, all-MiniLM-L6-v2 loads via transformers.js and embeds every chunk on your device. Rankings merge with reciprocal rank fusion.

  4. Graph expansion

    Top chunks activate their entities; activation spreads one hop across the graph. That's what lights up — and why it only returns what's actually written here.
