From Ohm's law to AI.

Case study · Data Squared · 2024 – now

Connecting an oil & gas company's data

The problem

Operations, maintenance, asset management and finance each ran on their own systems. Answering a question that crossed them meant pulling data by hand, and invoices were checked by reading documents line by line.

My part

  • Worked with client executives to define what the business needed.
  • Prototyped the data ingestion and integration layer, with traceability back to each source.
  • Built the unified knowledge graph across the four functions.
  • Designed the architecture for an unstructured-data loader: entity extraction and document classification for invoice verification and review.

Results

6 months → 4 weeksTime to integrate a new system, down from a typical six months. The prototype became a core component of the platform.
Hours → secondsInvoice analysis, down from hours of manual review, about 10× productivity for the client team on that workflow.
